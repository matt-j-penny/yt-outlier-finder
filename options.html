How to use

  1. FilterOpen the popup on YouTube to show only videos above an outlier score, subscriber or view range.
  2. Save a videoHover any video and click Save. Its outlier score comes with it.
  3. Save just the title or thumbnailRight-click a video and choose Save title or Save thumbnail to Content OS.

Outlier scores come from vidIQ Vision for YouTube, so install it alongside this extension.
